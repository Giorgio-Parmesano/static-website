Our Story

Where YouTube fame meets authentic Italian flavor

From the Screen to the Kitchen

La Tavola is the brainchild of Giorgio Parmegiano, the up-and-coming YouTube sensation who captivated millions with his passionate, no-nonsense approach to Italian cooking. What started as a YouTube channel filming recipes in a tiny apartment kitchen quickly exploded into a culinary movement -- and La Tavola is where that movement comes to life.

Giorgio grew up in Parma, surrounded by the aromas of his nonna's kitchen, where handmade pasta and slow-simmered sauces were a daily ritual. After moving to New York, he started sharing those family recipes online, and his infectious energy and uncompromising dedication to authenticity struck a chord with food lovers around the world.

"Respect the ingredients, honor the tradition, and never cut corners." — Giorgio Parmegiano

Our Philosophy

Giorgio's philosophy is simple: respect the ingredients, honor the tradition, and never cut corners. At La Tavola, we partner with local farms for seasonal produce, import specialty items directly from Italy, and make our pasta fresh every day. If you've watched Giorgio's videos, you know he won't settle for anything less than the real thing -- and neither will we.

We also believe that dining is about more than just food. It's about gathering around the table with family and friends, sharing stories, and creating memories. That same warmth and energy you feel watching Giorgio's channel is exactly what you'll find when you walk through our doors.

The Team

Giorgio leads the kitchen himself, bringing the same passion and creativity that made his channel a hit. He's joined by a handpicked team of cooks who share his vision, knowledgeable servers who can walk you through every dish, and a dedicated sommelier curating a wine list featuring both classic Italian estates and exciting emerging producers.

Whether you discovered Giorgio through his viral carbonara video or you're trying La Tavola for the first time, we promise you the same thing: honest, soulful Italian food made with love. No shortcuts, no gimmicks -- just great cooking.

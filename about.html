Our Story

A passion for Italian cooking since 1987

From Naples to New York

La Tavola was founded in 1987 by Chef Marco Rossi, who brought his family's culinary traditions from Naples to the heart of New York City. What started as a small trattoria with just ten tables has grown into one of Little Italy's most beloved dining destinations.

Chef Marco learned to cook in his grandmother's kitchen, where fresh ingredients and time-honored techniques were the foundation of every meal. He carried those values across the Atlantic, determined to share the authentic flavors of Southern Italy with a new audience.

Our Philosophy

At La Tavola, we believe that great food starts with great ingredients. We partner with local farms for seasonal produce, import specialty items directly from Italy, and make our pasta fresh every day. Our menu changes with the seasons to ensure that every dish showcases ingredients at their peak.

We also believe that dining is about more than just food. It's about gathering around the table with family and friends, sharing stories, and creating memories. That's why we've designed our space to feel warm, inviting, and unhurried -- a true Italian table where everyone is welcome.

The Team

Today, La Tavola is led by Chef Marco's daughter, Sofia Rossi, who trained at the Culinary Institute of America before apprenticing in restaurants across Tuscany and Emilia-Romagna. Sofia brings a modern sensibility to the restaurant while honoring the traditions that made La Tavola special from the beginning.

Our team includes passionate cooks, knowledgeable servers, and a dedicated sommelier who curates our wine list featuring both classic Italian estates and emerging producers. Together, we work to make every visit to La Tavola a memorable experience.
